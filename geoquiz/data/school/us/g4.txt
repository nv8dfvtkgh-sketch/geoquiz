## ela

Which sentence uses a metaphor? | The classroom was a zoo. | The classroom was like a zoo. | The class went to the zoo. | Zoos are fun.

Which is a relative pronoun? | who | he | them | my

Which sentence uses the progressive tense? | I am reading a book. | I read a book. | I will read. | I had read.
Which word should be capitalized? | Atlantic Ocean | ocean | river | lake

What is a primary source? | a firsthand account, like a diary | an encyclopedia | a textbook summary | a movie review
What is the difference between first and third person narration? | first uses "I", third uses "he/she/they" | there is none | first is longer | third uses "I"
